Holding Firm

Volvo EX90 2025: The Quiet Value-Holder

While most 2025 EVs have shed value quickly, the EX90 has moved the other direction. Every 2025 trim moved up this cycle. CarGurus data showed Performance Plus averaging ~$58,900 and Ultra configs clustering at $57–65K — well above where we had them.

EX90 2025 Trim Old Range New Range Mid Δ
Twin Motor Plus $47–55K $48–60K +$3K
Twin Motor Ultra $50–58K $57–60K +$4.5K
Twin Motor Perf. Plus $50–58K $55–63K +$5K
Twin Motor Perf. Ultra $53–63K $57–65K +$3K
Why is the EX90 holding? Supply is a big factor — Volvo’s production ramp has been slow, so there just aren’t many used units available. The 2025 was the first true mass-availability year, and most buyers bought to keep. There’s also a quality story: the EX90 has received strong long-term reliability signals and the Harman Kardon / Bowers & Wilkins audio setups are genuinely differentiating at this price point.
Repriced Down

Lucid Gravity Grand Touring: First Real Market Data

We’ve been carrying an estimated $96–101K for the Grand Touring since it first hit the used market. This cycle, KBB private-party data gave a clearer anchor: $82–88K. CarGurus asking prices averaged higher (~$109K), but those are dealer listings with markup. The new range reflects where actual transactions are clearing.

−$7K

Lucid Gravity 2026 Grand Touring

Pre-owned range: $96–101K → $85–97K  ·  KBB private party $82–88K; dealer avg $109K

Context: The Grand Touring at $85–97K represents roughly 20–30% off its $113K MSRP in 12–18 months. That’s steep depreciation, typical for debut-year luxury EVs from emerging brands. The Touring trim still has no meaningful used market (confirmed no-change), and the Dream Edition hasn’t appeared in used listings at all.
Softening

Rivian Tri Max: Peak Configs Depreciating Faster

KBB resale data this cycle showed both Tri Max configurations have moved down meaningfully. The 2025 Ascend Tri Max trade-in sits at $70–82K per KBB, with private party up to $88K — well below the $88–98K we had. The 2026 active listings on CarGurus clustered at $88–96K, not the $95–110K range.

R1S 2026 Tri Max

Pattern: The mid-range Rivian configs (Dual Large, Dual Max) have been more stable. The Tri Max premium at new-car time (~$20K over Dual Max) doesn’t carry over used — buyers hunting used R1S tend to prioritize range, and the Dual Max at 410 miles satisfies that. The Tri setup’s appeal is more about feel and towing than pure range.

Tesla Model X Plaid: 2024 Was Underpriced, 2026 Ceiling Came Down

Meanwhile the 2026 Plaid ceiling was too aggressive: the $175K upper bound was an outlier. The revised $118–158K is more defensible.

Data Cleanup

Mercedes EQS SUV 2026: Two Trims Renamed, Two Duplicates Removed

Mercedes quietly rebranded the EQS SUV lineup for the 2026 model year as part of a broader naming simplification. The EQS 450+ 4MATIC became the EQS 400 4MATIC and the EQS 580 4MATIC became the EQS 550 4MATIC. Same vehicles, same specs — Mercedes also cut prices ~$15K from the 2025 versions in the process (that correction happened last cycle).

The dataset had accumulated both the old and new names as separate entries. The old-named entries are now removed; their preowned rows were renamed to match. Total dataset count updated: 143 → 141 trims.

Old Name (removed) Replaced By MSRP
EQS 450+ 4MATIC (2026) EQS 400 4MATIC $89,950
EQS 580 4MATIC (2026) EQS 550 4MATIC $112,450
Note: The trim rename is purely cosmetic — no powertrain, range, or spec changes between the 450+ and 400 (or 580 and 550). Mercedes made this change across multiple models to reduce naming confusion between the all-electric EQS line and the hybrid versions. The Maybach EQS 680 name is unchanged.

On the Radar

Nissan Confirms a 3-Row BEV for 2028

Nissan formally confirmed a three-row fully electric SUV for the 2028 US market as part of their “The Arc” turnaround strategy. No model name, trim structure, or pricing has been announced. The vehicle will use next-generation NCM cells with ~50% higher energy density vs. the current Ariya, and will be built at Nissan’s Canton, Mississippi plant.
