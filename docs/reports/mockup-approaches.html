Approach C
Scrollytelling
Scroll-driven narrative where charts animate and transition as you read
Scroll Sections
In 2021, your only option was a Tesla Model X.
The 3-row EV market barely existed. By 2026, shoppers have 12 vehicles and 48 trims to choose from.
[ animated bar chart ]
Prices dropped as competition grew.
The median MSRP fell as mainstream brands entered the segment below $60k.
[ price trend line ]
Range went up. Charge times went down.
Average range improved 40% while DC fast charging times nearly halved.
[ dual-axis line ]
Strengths
  • Most engaging — reads like a visual essay
  • Perfect for blog embeds and social sharing
  • Guides users through insights progressively
Trade-offs
  • Significantly more complex to build (scroll observers, animations)
  • Harder to maintain — narrative text baked into the experience
  • Can feel heavy/slow on mobile
  • Overkill for a local-only first version
