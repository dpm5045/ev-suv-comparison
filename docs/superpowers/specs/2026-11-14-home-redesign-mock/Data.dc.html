The three-row EV market, in four charts.

We spent a lot of evenings in spreadsheets so you don’t have to. Here is what stood out, and then the whole dataset to poke at yourself.

1 · Choice

Five years ago there were six trims to choose from. Now there are 46.

In 2021 every one of them was a Tesla Model X. The jump came in 2024 and 2025, when Kia, Volvo, Cadillac and Volkswagen arrived.

3 · Range

Between $50,000 and $90,000, range barely moves.

Nine of 13 models land between 300 and 335 miles whatever they cost. Only the three with the biggest batteries break 400.
